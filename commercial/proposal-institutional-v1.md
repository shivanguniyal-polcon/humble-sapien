## What THS is

The Humble Sapien builds public-interest policy instruments and publishes them. Citizens, students, journalists, community groups and small non-profits run every tool free, permanently, with no account. Institutions that sell analysis or run administrations buy licences, and that revenue is what keeps the free tier unconditional.

Money buys rights, support and engineering time. It never buys a finding, an edit or a different answer. A panchayat office and a Big Four partner run the same code and get the same hashes.

## What is live today

Nine instruments run at humble-sapien.vercel.app. Three carry most of the institutional work.

**The Analysis Battery.** Eleven modules: cost-benefit analysis, cost-effectiveness analysis, distributional CBA, Markov cohort models, budget impact analysis, multi-criteria ranking, scenario runs against a calibrated PM-JAY reference model, stakeholder game theory, queueing models and Monte Carlo risk simulation.

**The Evaluation Engine.** Ordinary least squares, logit, instrumental variables (2SLS), difference-in-differences with event studies, sharp and fuzzy regression discontinuity, synthetic control and propensity score matching. It runs in the browser on built-in Indian Census and NFHS microdata or on your own CSV or JSON files.

**The Policy Simplifier.** Bilingual plain-language rewriting of statutes, notifications and contracts, with readability scoring, provenance tracking and five jurisdiction dictionaries: Indian bare Acts, UK statutory instruments, US CFR, EU regulations and Singapore statutes.

The other six are the stakeholder mapper, touchpoint maps, promise scorecards, the local-solutions knowledge base, the field-notes essays and the Data Commons. The PM-JAY fiscal model with its assumptions published, the verified escalation contacts and the promise scorecards form the public record behind the instrument set.

## What a licence buys

Rights, curation, time and capacity:

- The right to embed tool outputs in paid client deliverables, with attribution and the engine version stated.
- The audit manifest in every export.
- The four-question legal review gate and the methodology card.
- Volume terms for teams: banded seat counts, a named contact, a quarterly review call.

What no licence buys, in any tier: findings, conclusions or previews; a private dictionary or a private engine build; a warranty that any output is correct, current, in force or legally equivalent to its source; custody of your data (nothing is uploaded, so no data-sharing agreement is needed); legal advice.

A green flag means the wording changed mechanically. It does not mean a clause was classified correctly. The tool is an instrument for people who carry sign-off responsibility, not a substitute for them.

## Why the output survives review

Chatbox tools return text you have to trust. THS returns a run you can re-run.

- Every run emits an audit manifest: SHA-256 hashes over the canonical input, the parameters and the output, plus the engine version. Re-run the same files on the same version and the three hashes match byte for byte.
- Source text travels with every output, so a reviewer can check any sentence against what it rewrites.
- Results are stamped with the engine version that produced them, and that version keeps its terms. Upgrading is your choice. A licence change never reaches back to restrict work already generated under a published version.
- All computation runs in your browser. No input is uploaded to THS, stored by THS or visible to THS, so an engagement can start without a data-processing agreement.
- Parameters carry their verification status and the report prints it. Where the evidence is weak, the artefact says so.
