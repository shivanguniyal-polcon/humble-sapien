# THS Legal Simplifier — Rate Card v1

**Effective:** 2026-10-21 · **Currency:** USD, exclusive of taxes · **Term:** 12 months unless stated
**Positioning:** plain-language legal output with a defensible audit trail.

What money buys is **rights, curation, time and capacity**. It never buys a different
answer, a private engine, a preview of a finding, or a feature that the free tier does
not have. The engine the licensee runs is byte-identical to the engine a citizen runs.

**Every line excludes, without exception:** findings, conclusions, previews, feature
gates, private forks, and any warranty that a rewrite is correct, current, in force, or
legally equivalent to the source.

The right to embed Legal Simplifier outputs in paid client deliverables, with
attribution and the engine version stated.

| Line | Annual | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Small practice (1–10 fee earners) | **USD 12,000** | Boutique firms, chambers, solo-adjacent practices, specialist consultancies |
| Mid practice (11–50 fee earners) | **USD 18,000** | Regional firms, advisory boutiques, in-house teams at mid-size firms |
| Large practice + volume terms (50+) | **USD 25,000** | National and international firms, Big Four, multilateral programmes |

**Included:** right to embed reviewed outputs in paid deliverables; the audit manifest
in every export; the four-question legal review gate; the methodology card; jurisdiction
dictionaries (India bare Act, UK SI, US CFR, EU Regulation, Singapore statute).

**Not included:** a private dictionary, a private engine build, answers on your facts,
sign-off by a qualified lawyer, custody of your data (nothing is uploaded), training,
implementation, integration work.

**Volume terms (50+ fee earners):** banded seat counts, a named contact, a quarterly
review call. No exclusivity, no lock-in, no minimum commitment beyond the term.

**Term and renewal:** 12 months. Renewal is your choice. Rights generated under a
published version do not retract when the version moves.

## 2. Commons Plus — curation and versioning

What the licence does not cover: knowing when the source moved.

| Line | Annual / seat | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Newsroom, non-profit, academic | **USD 2,000** | Registered non-profits, newsrooms, universities, legal-aid clinics |
| Corporate | **USD 4,000** | Firms and companies buying curation for their own people |
| Redistribution rights | **USD 6,000** | Resellers, platforms, publishers repackaging the plain-language text |

**Included:** change alerts when a tracked instrument is amended or commences; the
comparative delta mode between two versions of the same instrument; the currency check
on every run; version pinning with a written notice before a version is retired.

**Not included:** the source of record (the gazette is the source of record), a legal
opinion on what the amendment means, automatic updates to your filed deliverables.

**Seat definition:** one named person. Seats are not concurrent licences and not
shared logins — there are no logins at all. A seat is a named person who receives the
curation.

## Payment

- **Institutions:** invoice on order. Domestic by UPI/NEFT; international by wire
  against an invoice in USD. FIRC/FIRA collected and filed. Invoice numbering
  `THS-YYYY-NNN`, GSTIN and SAC stated on the invoice where registration applies.
- **Individual advocates and small chambers (single seat, self-serve):** a one-time
  payment link, no invoice, no account. This is the fastest route to a working licence
  and it is deliberately the same route a supporter uses. If you need a GST invoice,
  take the institutional route above instead — an individual payment link cannot give
  you one.

## What a licence is not

It is not legal advice. It is not a certification that the source is current or in
force. It is not a guarantee that a rewrite means the same thing in every case. It is
not a substitute for a qualified lawyer's sign-off, and the tool says so in every
export. A green flag means the wording changed mechanically; it does not mean the
clause was classified correctly.
