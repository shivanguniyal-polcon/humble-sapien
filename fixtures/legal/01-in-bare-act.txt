Section 1. In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,— (1) "designated officer" means an officer appointed in writing by the Appropriate Government for the purposes of this Act; (2) "record" means any register, ledger or other document in which information relating to the services of the citizen is entered; (3) "specified authority" means such authority as may be specified by notification under section 8.

Section 2. The Appropriate Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, establish one or more District Service Commissions for the purposes of this Act, provided that no such Commission shall be established without prior consultation with the State Governments.

Section 3. Every designated officer shall be entitled to require any public servant to furnish such information as may be reasonably necessary for the purposes of this Act, and every public servant shall comply with such requisition.

Section 4. The Central Government may make rules for carrying out the purposes of this Act, and the rules may provide for— (a) the form and manner in which a record shall be maintained; (b) the period within which information shall be furnished; (c) the fees payable for the supply of a copy of a record; (d) the conditions subject to which a record may be withheld.

Section 5. Where a designated officer contravenes the provisions of this Act, such person shall be punished with imprisonment for a term which may extend to six months, and every such contravention shall also be an offence punishable with a fine of five thousand rupees for every such contravention.

Section 6. The provisions of section 5 shall not apply to any person who proves that he or she exercised all due diligence to prevent the commission of the contravention.

Section 7. Nothing contained in this Act shall have effect in so far as it is inconsistent with the provisions of the Right to Information Act, 2005.

Section 8. This Act shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may notify in the Official Gazette.
