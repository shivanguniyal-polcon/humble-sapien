§ 1026.35 Higher-priced mortgage loans.

(a) Definitions. For purposes of this section:
(1) "Covered institution" means any depository institution or Federal credit union that is subject to the requirements of this part.
(2) "Higher-priced mortgage loan" means a mortgage loan secured by a dwelling that is made to a consumer if the annual percentage interest rate exceeds the greater of—
(i) The average of the annual percentage interest rate for all mortgage loans for which the applicant obtained a mortgage loan from a covered institution in the ninety calendar days prior to the date the loan was made; or
(ii) The average of the annual percentage interest rate for all mortgage loans the applicant obtained in the ninety calendar days prior to the date the loan was made.
(3) "Qualifying ratio" means the ratio of total monthly debt payments, including the monthly payment on the new mortgage loan, to the consumer's monthly gross income.

(b) Prohibited acts. A covered institution shall not— (1) Advertise or otherwise make available a higher-priced mortgage loan; (2) Make a higher-priced mortgage loan unless the consumer's qualifying ratio is less than or equal to 43 percent; (3) Make a higher-priced mortgage loan on the basis of the age of the borrower.

(c) Exclusions. This section does not apply to— (1) A loan secured by a dwelling that is made to a consumer where the consumer's repayment obligation is secured by a guarantee from a governmental agency; (2) A loan that is made to a consumer on the basis of an equity line of credit secured by the dwelling; (3) A loan that is made to a consumer and is insured by the Federal Housing Administration.

(d) Exemption for certain loans. Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph (b), a covered institution shall not be subject to the requirements of that paragraph with respect to a mortgage loan if the covered institution has documented that it has made a reasonable, good faith determination that the consumer is able to repay the loan.

(e) Violations. A covered institution that violates this section is subject to a penalty of not more than the amount provided under section 8 of this part for each day that the violation continues.
