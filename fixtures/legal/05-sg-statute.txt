Community Facilities (Maintenance) Act 2026

1.—(1) This Act may be cited as the Community Facilities (Maintenance) Act 2026.

(2) This Act shall come into force on such date as the Minister may appoint by notification in the Gazette.

Part 1 — Preliminary

2. In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—
"common property" means the common property referred to in the Second Schedule;
"management corporation" means a management corporation registered under the Management Corporations Act 1994;
"prescribed" means prescribed by regulations made under this Act.

Part 2 — Duties of management corporations

3.—(1) Every management corporation shall—
(a) keep a register of the proprietors of the common property;
(b) maintain the common property in a state of good repair;
(c) establish a sinking fund to meet the costs of major works; and
(d) appoint an auditor and have the accounts of the corporation audited at least once in each financial year.

(2) A management corporation shall not incur any expenditure exceeding five thousand dollars in respect of any one item without the prior approval of the proprietors.

4. Every proprietor shall pay to the management corporation the contributions determined in accordance with the Second Schedule.

Part 3 — Enforcement

5.—(1) Where a management corporation fails to carry out any duty under section 3, the Minister may by written notice require the management corporation to carry out that duty within the period specified in the notice, and it shall be a contravention of this Act if the management corporation fails to comply with the notice.

(2) A management corporation that contravenes section 3(1)(a) or 3(1)(b) shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a fine not exceeding $10,000.

6. No proceedings shall be instituted against a management corporation under section 5(2) except with the consent of the Attorney-General.

Part 4 — Savings

7. Nothing in this Act shall affect the rights or obligations of any management corporation subsisting immediately before the commencement of this Act, and the First Schedule shall have effect for the purposes of this Part.
