## Lead

In December 2025, a reforms commission recommended reviving the Gujarat Health Recruitment Board — a body first promised in the 2022 manifesto, and still not verified as constituted nearly four years later. That single line is a perfect test of a distinction we care about: a recommendation to create something is not the same as the thing existing. So we went back to the four health promises and read each one against the record.

Before an election everything is possible: a health recruitment board, a health stack, a preventive healthcare fund, a genetics centre. These are real promises from a real manifesto in Gujarat, 2022. Health and child development took 26 per cent of all commitments.

Checked promise by promise against public evidence up to October 2026: the Health Recruitment Board is announced only, with a commission recommendation in December 2025 but constitution not verified. The Health Stack with a unique health ID is partly evidenced — about 5.25 crore ABHA accounts exist in Gujarat, but no Gujarat-specific one-stop stack is verified. The Dhanwantari Preventive Healthcare Fund and the Ahmedabad genetics centre: no public evidence found for either, as promised.

Notice the language, because it is the whole craft. "No public evidence found" is not the same as "it did not happen." It means the promise was made in public and the receipt was not. Governments should not be asked to whisper their achievements; they should be asked to leave paper. That gap — between the promise and the paper — is the subject, and this month it acquired a fresh example.

## Field note

The strictest status on our scorecards is deliberately a statement about receipts, not about reality. Anyone served by something that was never documented is missing from the paper by construction, and we say so on the card rather than pretending otherwise. This is the difference between accountability and accusation. We keep the vocabulary tight: delivered, partly evidenced, announced only, no public evidence found. Four words, and each one is a claim we can defend with a dated source.
