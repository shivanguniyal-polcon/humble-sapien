Regulator Obligation Monitor: published precision summary
==========================================================

Regulator Obligation Monitor: does the extractor clear the bar? — THS Research, October 2026

Findings as published:
1. Duty precision clears 90% on every blind holdout: 92.1%, 94.4% and 95.7% strict across three 50-row holdouts judged without the classifier labels. Strict means a hedged duty or a cut span scores as a miss; lenient it is 97-100%.
2. Duty recall is 100% on all four holdouts and 31/31 on three hand-coded circulars: the machine does not miss binding sentences that belong on the list.
3. Type accuracy — telling an action the firm must take apart from a composition rule or an eligibility grant — is 98-100%.
4. The applicability filter, which decides whose obligation each row is for a named entity profile, passed its hard gate with zero binding obligations silently dropped across 110 judged drops on two buyer profiles.
5. The one weak claim is the bearer tag at 41-59%: tagging which entity a duty falls on. That is the layer applicability filtering is built to take over, not a failure of finding or typing.

Method:
50 of the newest RBI circulars touching the NBFC family (June-October 2026), collected from the circular index with source URLs. The extractor runs the existing legal pipeline (offset-preserving sentence segmentation and clause classification) over a typed obligation schema. Precision is measured on a deterministic 200-row development sample plus three separate 50-row holdouts drawn from rows never judged and judged by hand against a stated rubric, without seeing the classifier labels. Recall is measured on three length-stratified circulars hand-coded in full. Every error class found on a holdout was documented and left unfixed rather than patched against the holdout, so each new number comes from a genuinely unseen set. Scoring is reproducible from the fixed gold in one command. This exhibit reports the measured accuracy of the extraction layer; it is not a shipped product, and the review-gated product shape remains an open founder decision.
