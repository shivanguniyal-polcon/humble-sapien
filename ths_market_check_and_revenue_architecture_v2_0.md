1. How the organisation can monetise layers of its public-interest technology (PIT) stack without compromising methodological or editorial independence.

6. **Promise Scorecards:** Empirical tracking of manifesto commitments against public evidence.
7. **Local-Solutions Knowledge Base:** Curated repository of decentralised civic interventions.
8. **Field-Notes Essay Series:** Deep methodological and field journalism.
9. **Policy Arcade:** Interactive civic simulation game.

## 3. The Dual-Mission Monetisation Framework

### 3.1 The Mission Wall
The Mission Wall separates public-welfare methodology from commercial revenue through four contractual and architectural rules:
1. **Contractual Non-Interference:** Every commercial licence, support contract, and commissioning agreement contains an explicit clause stating that the buyer cannot preview, delay, alter, or veto any public dataset, scorecard verdict, or algorithmic parameter.
2. **Identical Engine Guarantee:** A citizen in a rural district and a Senior Partner at a Tier-1 consultancy run the exact same browser calculation and receive the exact same SHA-256 model hash.
3. **Open-by-Default Artefacts:** Any module commissioned by a philanthropy, CSR foundation, or multilateral enters the public engine upon completion, with the funder's identity and contract value disclosed in the module manifest.
4. **Perpetual Free Citizen Access:** Tier 1 remains permanently free and account-free for non-commercial public use. Enforcement is contractual, not technical: the licence terms bind institutions, and the output carries the licensee's name.

### 3.3 The Five-Question Decision Gate (Updated)
Before any new product feature or revenue stream is approved, it must pass all five gates:
* **Q1 (Rights, Feeds, or Capacity?):** Does the line sell commercial usage rights, attributable provenance, curated data feeds, or engineering and training time, rather than changing the underlying analytical answer?
* **Q2 (The Wall Test):** Could a paying entity influence, preview, delay, or suppress a public finding, scorecard, or dataset under the proposed terms? *(If yes $\rightarrow$ Reject or rewrite terms).*
* **Q3 (Free Tier Integrity):** Does the citizen/civil-society tier remain free, account-free and computationally identical, with no technical restriction on its output?
* **Q4 (Browser Privacy Test):** Does the analytical computation remain 100% client-side so that sensitive user-uploaded evaluation datasets never sit on THS servers, with telemetry strictly restricted to network IP/ASN security and export license verification?
* **Q5 (Open-Default Test):** Does any commissioned tool or public-source data schema enter the public commons within a stated window?

### 5.2 Strategic Wedge: Pivoting from Generic ESG to Statutory CSR Impact Assessment
While SEBI's BRSR Core framework for India's top 1,000 listed companies is a major tailwind, corporate ESG software budgets are heavily captured by carbon-accounting and audit-workflow tools. 
* **The Higher-Converting Wedge:** Under Rule 8(3) of the Companies (CSR Policy) Rules, 2014, a company with an average CSR obligation of **₹10 crore or more** over the three preceding financial years must commission an **independent impact assessment** of CSR projects with an outlay of **₹1 crore or more**, completed at least one year before the assessment (MCA Circular 14/2021 applies this to projects completed on or after 22 January 2021). The report goes to the board and is annexed to the annual CSR report.
* **The budget is a permission, not a pool.** Rule 8(3)(c), as amended in 2022, lets a company book impact-assessment spending as CSR expenditure up to **2% of that year's total CSR spend or ₹50 lakh, whichever is higher**. A flat 2% on the reported ₹40,794 crore gives ₹816 crore; that arithmetic does not create an earmark, and the ₹50 lakh floor makes it a poor estimate of national assessment spending. Demand is real: KPMG's 2024 review found 91 of 114 eligible Nifty 250 companies disclosing assessment information. The sale is priced per assessment against what an agency would otherwise charge, not against a statutory pool.

## 8. Risks, Technical Realities & Guardrails

### 8.1 Technical Implementation of the Four Enforcement Controls (not approved)

**This section describes a proposal that conflicts with published promises.** The licensing page states "No account, no card, no expiry, no watermark", and the live code implements no watermark, no copy lock and no IP logging. Watermarking every export would also break the identical-output claim a licensee buys. IP/ASN retention for sales outreach processes personal data under the DPDP Act 2023 and needs a lawful basis and notice, which this design does not have. None of it should enter a build queue without a founder decision that explicitly withdraws the promise.

To ensure engineering effort on `humble-sapien.vercel.app` achieves maximum commercial compliance without breaking the browser-only calculation model, THS will deploy a three-layer technical lock:

1. **Dynamic Canvas & Export Watermarking (Solves the Screenshot Problem):**
   * *Reality Check:* Standard web browsers run inside an OS sandbox and cannot block OS-level screen capture shortcuts (`Win+Shift+S` or `Cmd+Shift+4`) or mobile phone cameras.
   * *The THS Engineering Solution:* Render a **repeating, semi-transparent SVG watermark layer directly over the live HTML5/Canvas charts and regression tables** in the browser (`UNLICENSED PUBLIC TIER — NOT FOR COMMERCIAL CONSULTING — IP: [XXX.XX.XX.XX]`). If an analyst takes an OS screenshot, the non-commercial watermark and their IP/timestamp are permanently baked into the image.
   * *Clean Export Unlock:* Only when a registered Tier 2 or Tier 3 user enters their cryptographic Licence Key (or accesses from a whitelisted corporate/campus IP block) does the Download engine generate a clean, client-ready PDF/PNG stamped with their official institutional badge and SHA-256 verification URL (`humble-sapien.vercel.app/verify/[HASH]`).
2. **DOM Copy-Lock (`user-select: none` + Clipboard Intercept):**
   * Disable right-click context menus, CSS text selection on regression/CBA output tables, and intercept `copy`/`cut` DOM events so users must route all outputs through the watermarked **Download** button.
3. **Server-Side Edge IP / ASN Telemetry (Zero Dataset Custody):**
   * While user-uploaded CSVs and econometric calculations execute 100% locally in WebAssembly/JavaScript (never touching THS servers), edge middleware logs the **autonomous system number (ASN) and corporate IP range** (via IPinfo / MaxMind) whenever a user triggers a calculation or watermarked download.
   * **Automated Sales Trigger:** When the edge log detects $\ge 10$ exports from an unregistered corporate IP block (e.g., KPMG, Shardul Amarchand, Sattva, or a corporate CSR desk), it automatically flags the organisation for the Commercial Owner to send a friendly compliance & licensing onboarding notice.
